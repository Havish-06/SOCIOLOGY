- **Best explanation of this outcome:** A crude line made for an "uncensored" room was published by the show itself
  to millions (collusion). It touched the family, which Indian public discourse treats as sacred, and fitted an
  existing legal category (obscenity). Political figures and a statutory commission made the accusation public
  within days, which turned an online storm into a multi-state legal process; the process itself, not any verdict,
  became the punishment. The scale and the speaker's prominence made the case a template for later controversies.
- **Topic:** family and parents. The crude genre mattered: an obscenity frame gives the state a ready legal route,
  which dark jokes on the same show (raina_2026) did not.
- **Rival explanations ruled out, and why:** election timing (no state election in Assam or Maharashtra at the time;
  not supported); a coordinated campaign (not yet testable; pending comment audit).
