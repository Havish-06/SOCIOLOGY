# Clips to watch and transcribe — Team K

The case drafts were written from news and legal reports only. Before checking a draft, the owner **watches the
full original performance** (not only the viral seconds) and pastes its transcript. Links found with the YouTube
Data API on 2026-11-09; the owner confirms each one is the right video.

## How to do it (per case, about 20–40 minutes)

1. **Do not open `case.md` yet.**
2. Open the **Watch** link(s). Watch the whole set, or at least 3–5 minutes before and after the joke.
3. Get the transcript: under the video, click **...more** → **Show transcript** → in the transcript panel's ⋮ menu,
   **Toggle timestamps** off → select all the transcript text → copy. Paste it into
   `data/transcripts/<case_id>__<video_id>.txt` (git-ignored) or straight into the chat with the AI.
   No "Show transcript" button (common for Telugu, Tamil and short clips)? Write down the key lines by hand.
4. Write your **first read** in `independent_analysis/first_read.csv` (PLAN.md, Step 5.2): what the joke was, what came
   before and after it, how the room reacted (laughter, silence, heckling), how the viral clip was cut, topic,
   direction, what exactly angered people, how it ended.
5. Only then open `case.md`, compare it with your first read, and fix it.

Legend: **Watch** = the performance itself (original or reupload; transcribe this). *Context* = news or the
comedian's response (optional, for understanding).
