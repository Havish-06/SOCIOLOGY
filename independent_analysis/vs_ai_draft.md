# Final analysis vs the team's AI draft

The team's AI drafts (`cases/<case_id>/case.md`, 9 Nov; deleted on 14 Nov, still in the git history) were written from news and legal reports only. The final
analysis here was made independently from the clip transcripts plus fresh research, then checked point by point
against those drafts; every disagreement was settled against the transcripts and sources (section M of each file).

## In numbers

How often the independent analysis matched the draft **before** settling, and how often the final coding matches it
(computed on 10 Nov; 24 cases):

| Field | Independent = draft | Final = draft |
|---|---|---|
| Outcome type | 19 | 20 |
| Direction (up / down / ...) | 20 | 23 |
| Collapse type (collusion / collision) | 22 | 22 |
| Highest stigma step | 20 | 21 |
| Courtesy stigma | 19 | 20 |

- **Outcome changed in 4 cases**: fernandes_2024 (both → extra-legal), jain_univ_2023 (legal → both), kamra_2018
  (extra-legal → legal, administrative), parithabangal_2025 (both → legal). In more_2026 the draft was right and the
  independent analysis was corrected (both → legal).
- **The draft's big-picture codes were mostly right; its errors sit where a news-only analysis is weakest**: the
  joke itself, its source, and its context. It misplaced the source of the joke in 4 cases (virli_2026,
  raina_sma_2025, more_2025, faruqui_2024) and misread the joke in others (sachdeva_2025, katikala_2026).
- The independent analysis also made errors that the check corrected (e.g. more_2026's single-source cancellation,
  latent_2025 and raina_2026 direction, a Delhi FIR in virdas_2021 that police said was never registered).
