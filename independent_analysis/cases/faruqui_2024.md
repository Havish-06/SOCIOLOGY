## Summary

At a show in the Mumbai area, Munawar Faruqui asked whether anyone in the audience had travelled to the show. A man said he came from Taloja (Navi Mumbai/Raigad, outside Mumbai). Faruqui riffed that people from there tell their villages they "live in Bombay", and added "ये कोकनी लोग चू[तिया] बनाते सबको" ("these Konkani people fool everyone"), using a vulgar Hindi word. The clip spread on X around 11–12 August 2024, where MNS-supporting accounts tagged MNS leaders and asked them to act. BJP MLA Nitesh Rane, a politician from the Konkan region, posted a Marathi video. He called Faruqui a "green snake" who could be sent to Pakistan, said "we know his address", told the public not to follow him, and demanded an apology. A Shinde-faction Shiv Sena figure reportedly offered a cash reward to anyone who beat him. On 12 August Faruqui posted an apology video. He called it crowd work taken out of context, said the man he joked about had enjoyed it, and said "मैं आप सबसे दिल से माफी मांगता हूं, जय हिंद जय महाराष्ट्र". Rane reposted it with a warning of "direct action" if he spoke about Hindus or Konkanis again. No FIR, complaint or cancellation was found. The case is **extra-legal only** (threats and a pressured apology). It was short-lived: about three days. The clip itself shows that the show was **not in Taloja**, as many outlets and the case description say. The Taloja man was a visitor who had travelled to it.
