## Summary

On 12 June 2026, four days after Pranit More's "Rs 370 biryani" clip went viral, an old clip of Delhi comedian Madhur Virli (an IIT Delhi graduate known for sex and relationship material) resurfaced. In it he says that of "10 rape cases", nine are "only rape" and one is "murder after rape". He imagines that case happens when, just after the rape, the victim asks the man whether he will not cuddle. News reports attributed it to his 2024 special *Love & Latex*. **The joke is not in the version of *Love & Latex* now on YouTube.** That version (27:57) was already the same length on 8 June 2026, before the controversy. Virli's apology (14–15 June) says he realised the joke was wrong "soon after performing" it and "took it down at that time". The best reading is that the bit was in an earlier cut or clip of his 2024 material and was later removed, while copies kept circulating. A third-party clip of it was online by June 2025, and a different rape joke of his had circulated as a reel since June 2024. The National Commission for Women issued a suo motu notice and summoned him. On 22 June it rejected his apology, alongside More's. In October 2026 Maharashtra Cyber summoned him over "obscene" content on a woman's complaint. No FIR against him has been reported. Outcome: **legal** (statutory body and police summons). This is a collision case: an old, self-removed joke was pulled into a wave started by someone else.

- Topic(s): gender_sexuality; death_violence_tragedy.
- Genre(s): dark; crude_shock.
- Why this genre: the subject is grave (rape and murder), and the laugh comes from that gravity. The punchline inverts the crime into a date, so the incongruity depends on the horror of the act. It meets both parts of the Willinger definition. Its shock value (casual statistics followed by a romantic-comedy line) also makes it crude_shock.
- Target: rape victims; women who are victims of sexual violence. The punchline gives the victim a line that "explains" her murder, so the butt is the victim, not the rapist. Direction: **down**.
- Form: scripted_standup.
- Main technique: irony_sarcasm (incongruous romantic line in a crime scenario), introduced with a pseudo-statistic.

- Circulation: **collision**. Third parties resurfaced the clip with hostile captions ("Rape is a joke or fun for Madhur virli"; "MADHUR VIRLI being INSENSITIVE & Cracking Ra*pe Jokes"). Virli says he had removed it.
  - who first spread it beyond the original audience: if the bit was in the original cut, Virli himself in 2024. The 2025 and 2026 spread came from third-party accounts and aggregator channels. The 2026 trigger account is not identified.
  - months between performance and controversy: about 28 (Feb 2024 → Jun 2026). About 16 months between the earlier 2025 resurfacing and the 2026 wave.
  - caption of the most-viewed circulating version: accusatory (examples above). The news title was "…Faces Backlash Over Viral Rape Joke Clip After '370 Ki Biryani' Controversy".
- Reception: online public, women's-rights voices, Uorfi Javed, a commentator calling on Delhi Police to act (13 June), the NCW, and later Maharashtra Cyber on a woman's complaint. Objector distance: social (speaking for victims and women) and temporal (about 2 years).
- Societal: the "Rs 370 biryani" row (8–16 June 2026) had made consent and misogyny in comedy a national topic, and the NCW was already acting on More. Virli's clip was framed explicitly "after the 370 biryani controversy".

## E. Reaction

- **Best explanation of this outcome:** The joke's content (rape-murder as a punchline that blames the victim) is the kind that fails the benign-violation test outside the room. But it had already circulated in 2024–2025 without consequence. What changed in June 2026 was the wave started by the More row. That gave the clip a hostile frame ("after 370 biryani") and gave the NCW a template, so it processed Virli alongside More. Context collapse (collision) plus a moral-panic-style wave and platform amplification explain the escalation. The absence of any organised offended group explains why it stayed at a statutory inquiry rather than FIRs or street action.
- **Topic:** sexual violence against women. Not a sacred object in the fixed list, but protected by a statutory body. Genre mattered: as dark comedy whose laugh comes from the gravity of rape, it was easy to quote in one line and hard to defend.
- **Rival explanations ruled out:** (1) Fame: Virli is mid-sized, and his larger 2024 special went unchallenged for two years. (2) A political actor: none. (3) Religion or caste: not involved. (4) New content: no. The material was old and apparently already removed.
