## Summary

Neville Shah, a Mumbai stand-up comedian, performed a bit in a 2016 set that was later streamed on Amazon Prime Video. In it he talks about his mother's illness: a doctor who walks with a limp is either "Dr. House" (the genius TV doctor with a limp) or a "quota admission", and "clearly this doctor was quota". A 59-second clip circulated on Twitter around 15–16 May 2021, about five years after the taping, and was criticised by anti-caste users and collectives as casteist (it mocks doctors admitted through reservation) and ableist (it treats the limp as a sign of incompetence). Shah apologised on Twitter about a day later, citing the death of his mother in 2016 and acknowledging his privilege. Critics called the apology inadequate, and the episode set off a week of call-outs of other comedians' old caste, sexist and Islamophobic jokes and tweets. No complaint, FIR, cancellation or threat against Shah was found in the sources. The case is an example of a resurfaced clip (collision) judged by the group the joke targets, ending online only, inside a wider May 2021 wave of caste call-outs.
