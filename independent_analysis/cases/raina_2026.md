## Summary
In episode 5 of *India's Got Latent* season 2, released on 28 August 2026 on YouTube and Netflix, host Samay Raina introduced guest judge Sharon Verma, a comedian from Bihar who had been a contestant in season 1. The introduction became a reciprocal "you're so ..." roast. Raina joked that Verma is "so Bihari she thinks Ebola means someone said something" and that she is a Bihari who came to Mumbai for a job. Verma answered that Raina is "so Kashmiri he loves getting stoned" and, as the closing line, "at least I left my state by my choice", which was read as a reference to the 1990 exodus of Kashmiri Pandits. Raina is himself a Kashmiri Pandit. From 29 August, clips drew online criticism and solicited statements from politicians of several parties: BJP MLA Ram Kadam (to IANS), Bihar's Education Minister Mithilesh Tiwari, Shiv Sena (UBT)'s Anand Dubey, Congress's Harish Rawat, PDP's Syed Tajamul Islam and others, plus the Jammu and Kashmir Peace Forum. As of 9 November 2026 no complaint, FIR, summons, threat or cancellation has been found, and neither performer responded. Verma returned as a judge a week later. The outcome is **online only** (with political statements). The episode was the show's most-viewed in its last ten. The contrast with latent_2025 (same show, same host) is the key comparison: this time no fixed sacred object was touched, the jokes were reciprocal and partly self-directed, and nobody took the step from statement to complaint.
