## Summary

In July 2018 a local organisation booked the CC Mehta Auditorium of the Maharaja Sayajirao (MS) University of Baroda, Vadodara, for a Kunal Kamra show on 11 August 2018. The week before, 11 alumni wrote to the vice-chancellor. Their letter did not name Kamra but called him an "anti-national flag-bearer youth comedian", said he had "mimicked [the] National Anthem" and "openly supported Tukde-Tukde Gang", and suspected an "ideological conspiracy to pollute the minds of Barodian youth ahead of 2019". On Saturday 21 July 2018 the vice-chancellor's office directed the auditorium to cancel the show. The coordinator told the Indian Express it was because of content that was "anti-national and controversial", and told PTI it was about "the security of the venue". Kamra learned of it from the news and tweeted a joke about his day off. No FIR, threat or violence was reported. The case is an **administrative cancellation by a statutory public university**, coded `legal` (administrative), triggered by a letter from private individuals. The objection was to Kamra's **political persona**, not to an identifiable joke. His 2017 "Patriotism & the Government" video, the best-known source of that persona, contains a bit mocking the idea that JNU students threaten the nation and a running gag about using soldiers as an excuse. It does not contain any legible national-anthem bit.
