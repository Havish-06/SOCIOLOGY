## Summary

Marathi/Hindi stand-up comedian Pranit More made jokes about debut actor Veer Pahariya (*Sky Force*, January 2025). Pahariya is the grandson of Sushilkumar Shinde, a former Maharashtra chief minister and Union home minister whose political base is Solapur. On 2 February 2025, after More's show at a Solapur venue (reported as 24K Kraft Brewzz), about 10–12 men posing as fans waited for photos and then punched and kicked him. One reportedly said "Agli baar Veer Pahariya baba pe joke maarke dikha" ("make a joke on Veer Pahariya baba next time and see"). More's team posted about it on Instagram. Pahariya denied any involvement, condemned the violence and apologised. Police registered a case against 10–12 people on a complaint from the venue owner. No legal action was taken against More. The outcome is **extra-legal**: assault and threat by self-described fans. A month later More released "Solapur's Got Latent", a set that turns the attack into comedy and says he will return to Solapur. In it he says the joke the attackers were angry about was **not** told at the Solapur show but **earlier**, and that the attackers played him a **reel** of it. The original joke is in none of the transcripts, and none of the news reports describe it.
