## Summary

In mid-June 2024 ThePrint reported that Jains in Old Delhi had dressed as Muslims and paid about Rs 15 lakh for 124 goats so that the goats would not be sacrificed at Bakrid. Daniel Fernandes, a Mumbai-based English-language stand-up comedian, posted a short topical video about the story. In it he said the "rescued" goats would breed and the Jains would end up phoning a Muslim seller to ask when Eid was, and he set these Jains against Jains who own meat-export businesses. The video drew angry comments from Jain and Hindu users within hours. Fernandes posted a statement, deleted the video and apologised. On 29 June the BJP MLA T. Raja Singh posted a video telling him to cancel his Hyderabad show that afternoon or be beaten by party workers, and he cited the 2022 threats against Munawar Faruqui. The show was called off, and in all four shows in Chennai and Hyderabad were reportedly cancelled. Police said the organisers had cancelled on their own and had never asked for security. No FIR against Fernandes was found. The outcome is **extra-legal**: a politician's threat, the cancellations and a pressured apology. The legal system was not used.

- **Best explanation of this outcome:** The joke itself provoked ordinary online anger from the targeted community, and an apology was already under way. It ended extra-legally because a Hindutva MLA with an established local repertoire of threatening comedians' Hyderabad shows took it up on the day of the show. He framed Jain hurt as Hindu hurt and placed responsibility for "the atmosphere" on the police. The organisers, with no police security requested, chose to cancel. Strategic offence plus a local precedent, together with a low-power target (a mid-tier English comic touring small venues), explains the outcome better than the content of the joke.
- **Topic:** religious practice (Jain ahimsa) set against a Muslim festival, which made it two-religion and inter-communal. The meat-export line turned an ironic observation into a factual-sounding accusation, which narrowed the comic frame.

A small fan base may make cancellation easier, which is consistent with but not proof of that rival. *Platform*: the same Instagram/YouTube outrage happens often without cancellations, and the decisive step was the MLA's video. *Comedian's identity*: no source links his identity to the reaction.
