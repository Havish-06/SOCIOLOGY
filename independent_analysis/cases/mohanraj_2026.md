## Summary

On 15 June 2026, a week into the "Rs 370 biryani" row, an X account posted an old clip of Aishwarya Mohanraj, a Mumbai-based English-language comedian and YouTuber who was a contestant on the first season of Amazon Prime Video's *Comicstaan* (July–August 2018). In the bit she calls her mother "the epitome of misandry… the Haryana for male foeticide". She says that when her brother was born her mother "cried like a baby" because "the nurse wasn't letting her cut his something", and that her mother is her role model because "some day I want to fulfil her unfulfilled dream, when I have a son". Critics, many using a "double standards" frame, called it hatred of male children and asked whether a man would get away with the reverse. Others defended it as dark comedy. Some attacks shifted to her personal life (her marriage, clothes and the absence of a mangalsutra or sindoor). News sites (India TV, Daily Jagran, The Lallantop) bundled her with More and Virli. No complaint, FIR, NCW action, cancellation or statement by Mohanraj was found. Outcome: **online only**. The case shows the wave reaching a woman comedian's joke that runs in the opposite gender direction. The clip's key context, an inversion of female foeticide ("Haryana"), was mostly absent from the outrage.
