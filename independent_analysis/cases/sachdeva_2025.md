## Summary
Swati Sachdeva, a Delhi-raised Hindi stand-up comedian who says on stage that she is bisexual, posted the 20-minute set "Family First" on her YouTube channel on 20 March 2025. In the first three minutes she tells a story about her mother finding her vibrator. The mother tries to be a "cool mom", cannot say the word ("gadget", "toy"), and finally uses the incident to push marriage. About eight days later (28 March), a clip of the bit spread on X. Users called it "shameless", "filth" and disrespectful to parents, compared it to the *India's Got Latent* parents remark (latent_2025), and some tagged Delhi Police, MeitY and the PMO asking for action. Entertainment and digital news sites (DNA, Republic, Filmibeat, IBTimes, Free Press Journal) covered it on 29–31 March. No complaint, FIR, commission notice, threat or cancellation has been found, and Sachdeva did not respond. The row faded within about a week. Outcome: **online only**. The case matters because it touches the **same sacred object as latent_2025** (parents, with sexual content) in the same weeks, yet no one took it further. In the full set the bit is about mother–daughter awkwardness and **marriage pressure on a bisexual daughter**, a frame missing from all news coverage.
