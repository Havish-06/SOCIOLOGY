# Recoding for the new sacred object: collective trauma (2026-11-12)

**Definition** (PROJECT.md §4, CODEBOOK B4): a specific, widely remembered event of mass violence, atrocity or displacement against a group,
and its victims. Examples: the Kashmiri Pandit exodus, a terror attack, communal riots, a caste killing that became a public cause.
**Not included:** generic categories of victims (rape victims in general, disabled people, sick children), and attacks on a single person.

**How to record it:** in the joke (the joke is about the event or its victims) · mentioned but not mocked (it appears; the joke targets
something else) · claimed by objectors (the joke isn't about it, but objectors said it was).

Each owner confirms or corrects the line for their cases. Then the `sacred_object` field in each case's Final coding is updated and
`comparison.csv` rebuilt.
